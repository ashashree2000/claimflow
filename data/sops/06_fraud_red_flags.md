# SOP-FRD Fraud red flags (SYNTHETIC)

> Synthetic claims-handling procedure written for the ClaimFlow demonstration (fictional insurer Northwind Mutual).

### SOP-FRD-1.1 Red flags
Red flags include a claim soon after the policy start date, dates that do not match each other, and a loss reported long after it was discovered.

### SOP-FRD-1.2 Handling red flags
Red flags are noted in the triage packet for the reviewer. They do not change the intake steps and are not a reason to delay the acknowledgement.
