# SOP-COM Communication rules (SYNTHETIC)

> Synthetic claims-handling procedure written for the ClaimFlow demonstration (fictional insurer Northwind Mutual).

### SOP-COM-1.1 Plain language
Write to claimants in plain language and explain what happens next.

### SOP-COM-1.2 Personal data
Do not copy personal data such as phone numbers, email addresses or ID numbers into internal notes or logs; refer to the claim number instead.
