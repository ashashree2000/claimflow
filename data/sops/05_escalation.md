# SOP-ESC Escalation and human review (SYNTHETIC)

> Synthetic claims-handling procedure written for the ClaimFlow demonstration (fictional insurer Northwind Mutual).

### SOP-ESC-1.1 Events the wording does not address
If the policy wording does not address the event in the claim, do not decide coverage at intake. Mark the claim as needing human review and refer it to a senior handler.

### SOP-ESC-1.2 Missing or conflicting facts
If the notice does not give enough facts to identify what happened, or the facts conflict, mark the claim as needing human review.

### SOP-ESC-1.3 Decisions are recommendations
A coverage view given at intake is a recommendation. A claims handler approves or rejects every recommendation, and the decision is recorded.
