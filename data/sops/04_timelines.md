# SOP-TIM Service timelines (SYNTHETIC)

> Synthetic claims-handling procedure written for the ClaimFlow demonstration (fictional insurer Northwind Mutual).

### SOP-TIM-1.1 Acknowledgement
Send the claimant an acknowledgement within 24 hours of receiving the FNOL.

### SOP-TIM-1.2 Document follow-up
If a requested document has not been received within 3 days of the request, send the claimant a reminder.

### SOP-TIM-1.3 Assignment
Assign a surveyor (motor and property) or a medical reviewer (health) within 2 days of the coverage check.
