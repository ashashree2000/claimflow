# SOP-DOC Document checklist (SYNTHETIC)

> Synthetic claims-handling procedure written for the ClaimFlow demonstration (fictional insurer Northwind Mutual).

### SOP-DOC-1.1 One consolidated request
Request all documents that are still missing in one message, as soon as the coverage check is completed.

### SOP-DOC-1.2 Recording documents
Record every document received in the claim file on the day it arrives.

### SOP-DOC-1.3 No repeat requests
Before requesting a document, check the claim file. Never request a document that has already been received or was attached to the notice.

### SOP-DOC-2.1 Motor checklist
Motor claims need the claim form, the driver's driving licence, the vehicle registration certificate, photos of the damage and a repair estimate. Theft claims need the claim form, the driving licence, the registration certificate and the police report instead.

### SOP-DOC-2.2 Property checklist
Property claims need the claim form, photos of the damage, a repair estimate and proof of ownership of damaged contents. Burglary and theft claims also need the police report.

### SOP-DOC-2.3 Health checklist
Health claims need the claim form, the discharge summary, the itemised hospital bill and the insured person's ID proof.
