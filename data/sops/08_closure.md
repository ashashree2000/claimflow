# SOP-CLS Closure (SYNTHETIC)

> Synthetic claims-handling procedure written for the ClaimFlow demonstration (fictional insurer Northwind Mutual).

### SOP-CLS-1.1 Closing a claim
A claim is closed after the decision has been recorded and any payment has been made.

### SOP-CLS-1.2 Closure letter
Send the claimant a closure letter that states the decision and the clauses it relies on.
