# SOP-VER Verification steps (SYNTHETIC)

> Synthetic claims-handling procedure written for the ClaimFlow demonstration (fictional insurer Northwind Mutual).

### SOP-VER-1.1 Order of verification
Verify the policy status and complete the coverage check before assigning a surveyor or a medical reviewer.

### SOP-VER-1.2 Verify once
Each verification step is performed once per claim. Later steps rely on the recorded result; a step is repeated only if the claim facts change, and the reason is recorded.

### SOP-VER-1.3 Recording results
Record the outcome of every verification step in the claim file with the time and the handler's name.
