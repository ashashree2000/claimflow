# Individual Health Policy Wording (SYNTHETIC)

> Synthetic document written for the ClaimFlow demonstration. Northwind Mutual is a fictional insurer. Not a real policy.

## HLTH-DEF-1 Definitions
The words below have the same meaning wherever they appear in this policy.

### HLTH-DEF-1.1 Hospitalisation
Admission to a hospital as an inpatient for at least 24 consecutive hours on a doctor's advice.

### HLTH-DEF-1.2 Day-care procedure
A procedure that needs less than 24 hours in hospital, limited to cataract surgery, dialysis, chemotherapy, radiotherapy and lithotripsy.

### HLTH-DEF-1.3 Pre-existing condition
Any condition, illness or injury that was diagnosed by a doctor, or for which the insured person received treatment or medication, within 36 months before the policy start date.

### HLTH-DEF-1.4 Policy start date
The date the insured person was first covered by this policy without a break in cover.

## HLTH-COV-2 What is covered
We cover the expenses in this section, subject to the limits in HLTH-LIM-3, the exclusions in HLTH-EXC-4 and the conditions in HLTH-CON-5.

### HLTH-COV-2.1 Inpatient hospitalisation
We pay for room, nursing, surgery, doctors' fees, tests and medicines during hospitalisation (HLTH-DEF-1.1), subject to HLTH-EXC-4.1 and HLTH-CON-5.1.

### HLTH-COV-2.2 Day-care procedures
We pay for day-care procedures (HLTH-DEF-1.2), subject to the sub-limit in HLTH-LIM-3.3.

### HLTH-COV-2.3 Before and after hospitalisation
We pay for medical expenses for the same condition up to 30 days before admission and up to 60 days after discharge, when the hospitalisation is covered under HLTH-COV-2.1.

### HLTH-COV-2.4 Road ambulance
We pay up to Rs 3,000 per hospitalisation for road ambulance transport to hospital, when the hospitalisation is covered under HLTH-COV-2.1.

## HLTH-LIM-3 Limits

### HLTH-LIM-3.1 Sum insured
The most we pay in one policy year is the sum insured of Rs 5,00,000.

### HLTH-LIM-3.2 Room rent
We pay room rent up to 1% of the sum insured per day.

### HLTH-LIM-3.3 Cataract sub-limit
The most we pay for cataract surgery is Rs 40,000 per eye.

## HLTH-EXC-4 What is not covered
The exclusions in this section apply to every part of HLTH-COV-2 unless a clause says otherwise.

### HLTH-EXC-4.1 Pre-existing conditions
We do not pay for treatment of a pre-existing condition (HLTH-DEF-1.3) until the insured person has had 36 months of continuous cover. This exclusion is amended by HLTH-END-6.1.

### HLTH-EXC-4.2 Cosmetic treatment
We do not pay for cosmetic or aesthetic treatment, unless it is needed to repair damage caused by an accident or burns.

### HLTH-EXC-4.3 Outpatient treatment
We do not pay for consultations, tests or medicines that are not part of a hospitalisation or a day-care procedure.

### HLTH-EXC-4.4 First 30 days
We do not pay for any illness that first starts within 30 days of the policy start date. This does not apply to accidental injury.

### HLTH-EXC-4.5 Experimental treatment
We do not pay for experimental or unproven treatment.

## HLTH-CON-5 Conditions

### HLTH-CON-5.1 Notice of hospitalisation
We must be told of a hospitalisation within 7 days of admission.

### HLTH-CON-5.2 Original documents
Claims must be supported by the original discharge summary and itemised hospital bill.

## HLTH-END-6 Endorsements
The endorsements in this section form part of every policy issued under this wording.

### HLTH-END-6.1 Diabetes and hypertension
For diabetes and hypertension only, the 36-month period in HLTH-EXC-4.1 is reduced to 12 months.
