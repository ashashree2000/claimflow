# Home Insurance Policy Wording (SYNTHETIC)

> Synthetic document written for the ClaimFlow demonstration. Northwind Mutual is a fictional insurer. Not a real policy.

## PROP-DEF-1 Definitions
The words below have the same meaning wherever they appear in this policy.

### PROP-DEF-1.1 Home
The residential building at the address in the policy schedule, including its fixed fittings, floors, walls and roof.

### PROP-DEF-1.2 Contents
Household goods and personal belongings owned by the policyholder or their family and kept in the home.

### PROP-DEF-1.3 Unoccupied
The home is unoccupied when neither the policyholder nor any family member has lived in it for more than 30 consecutive days.

### PROP-DEF-1.4 Flood
Water entering the home from overflowing rivers, lakes or drains, or from the build-up of surface rainwater.

## PROP-COV-2 What is covered
We cover the losses in this section, subject to the limits in PROP-LIM-3, the exclusions in PROP-EXC-4 and the conditions in PROP-CON-5.

### PROP-COV-2.1 Fire and smoke
We pay for damage to the home and contents caused by fire, smoke, lightning or explosion.

### PROP-COV-2.2 Escape of water
We pay for damage to the home and contents caused by water escaping suddenly from a fixed water tank, pipe or appliance, subject to PROP-EXC-4.1 and PROP-EXC-4.2.

### PROP-COV-2.3 Storm and flood
We pay for damage to the home and contents caused by storm, high winds or flood, subject to PROP-EXC-4.3.

### PROP-COV-2.4 Burglary
We pay for contents stolen, and damage to the home caused, during a theft involving forcible and violent entry into the home, subject to PROP-EXC-4.4 and PROP-CON-5.2.

## PROP-LIM-3 Limits and excess

### PROP-LIM-3.1 Building limit
The most we pay for damage to the home is the building sum insured shown in the schedule.

### PROP-LIM-3.2 Contents limit
The most we pay for contents is the contents sum insured. Any single item worth more than Rs 50,000 is covered only if it is listed in the schedule.

### PROP-LIM-3.3 Escape of water excess
The policyholder pays the first Rs 10,000 of each claim under PROP-COV-2.2.

## PROP-EXC-4 What is not covered
The exclusions in this section apply to every part of PROP-COV-2 unless a clause says otherwise.

### PROP-EXC-4.1 Gradual deterioration
We do not pay for damage that happens gradually, including wear and tear, rot, damp, mould and water seeping slowly over weeks or months.

### PROP-EXC-4.2 Escape of water while unoccupied
We do not pay for escape of water damage that happens while the home is unoccupied (PROP-DEF-1.3).

### PROP-EXC-4.3 Flood
We do not pay for damage caused by flood (PROP-DEF-1.4). This exclusion is amended by PROP-END-6.1.

### PROP-EXC-4.4 Theft without forcible entry
We do not pay for theft or disappearance of contents where there is no sign of forcible and violent entry into the home.

### PROP-EXC-4.5 Deliberate damage
We do not pay for damage caused deliberately by the policyholder or a family member.

## PROP-CON-5 Conditions

### PROP-CON-5.1 Notice of claim
The policyholder must tell us about any loss within 14 days of discovering it.

### PROP-CON-5.2 Police report for burglary
A burglary must be reported to the police within 24 hours of being discovered.

### PROP-CON-5.3 Preventing further damage
The policyholder must take reasonable steps to prevent further damage after a loss.

## PROP-END-6 Endorsements
The endorsements in this section form part of every policy issued under this wording.

### PROP-END-6.1 Flood cover for the building
PROP-EXC-4.3 does not apply to damage to the home itself (PROP-DEF-1.1). It still applies to contents kept below ground level, such as in a basement or cellar.
