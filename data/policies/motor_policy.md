# Private Car Policy Wording (SYNTHETIC)

> Synthetic document written for the ClaimFlow demonstration. Northwind Mutual is a fictional insurer. Not a real policy.

## MOTOR-DEF-1 Definitions
The words below have the same meaning wherever they appear in this policy.

### MOTOR-DEF-1.1 Insured vehicle
The private car named in the policy schedule.

### MOTOR-DEF-1.2 Authorised driver
The policyholder, or any person driving with the policyholder's permission, who holds a valid driving licence for the class of vehicle at the time of the incident.

### MOTOR-DEF-1.3 Commercial use
Use of the insured vehicle for hire or reward, meaning carrying passengers or goods in return for payment, including work arranged through any digital platform.

### MOTOR-DEF-1.4 Market value
The manufacturer's listed selling price of the insured vehicle, reduced by depreciation for its age, at the time of the incident.

## MOTOR-COV-2 What is covered
We cover the losses in this section, subject to the limits in MOTOR-LIM-3, the exclusions in MOTOR-EXC-4 and the conditions in MOTOR-CON-5.

### MOTOR-COV-2.1 Accidental damage
We pay for damage to the insured vehicle caused by collision, overturning or impact with another object, subject to MOTOR-EXC-4.1 and MOTOR-EXC-4.2.

### MOTOR-COV-2.2 Theft
We pay for the loss of the insured vehicle, or of parts fitted to it, caused by theft, subject to MOTOR-CON-5.2.

### MOTOR-COV-2.3 Liability to third parties
We pay amounts the policyholder is legally liable to pay for damage to another person's property, or for bodily injury to another person, caused by the use of the insured vehicle. The limit is in MOTOR-LIM-3.3.

### MOTOR-COV-2.4 Windscreen and glass
We pay to repair or replace broken windscreen, window or sunroof glass of the insured vehicle. No excess applies. The limit is in MOTOR-LIM-3.2.

### MOTOR-COV-2.5 Fire and natural perils
We pay for damage to the insured vehicle caused by fire, explosion, lightning, storm, hail, flood or landslide.

## MOTOR-LIM-3 Limits and excess

### MOTOR-LIM-3.1 Own-damage limit and excess
For claims under MOTOR-COV-2.1, MOTOR-COV-2.2 and MOTOR-COV-2.5 the most we pay is the market value (MOTOR-DEF-1.4). The policyholder pays the first Rs 5,000 of each claim.

### MOTOR-LIM-3.2 Glass limit
The most we pay under MOTOR-COV-2.4 is Rs 25,000 per claim.

### MOTOR-LIM-3.3 Third-party property limit
The most we pay for damage to third-party property is Rs 7,50,000 per incident. Bodily injury to third parties has no limit, as required by law.

## MOTOR-EXC-4 What is not covered
The exclusions in this section apply to every part of MOTOR-COV-2 unless a clause says otherwise.

### MOTOR-EXC-4.1 Unauthorised driver
We do not pay for any loss while the insured vehicle is driven by a person who is not an authorised driver (MOTOR-DEF-1.2).

### MOTOR-EXC-4.2 Commercial use
We do not pay for any loss while the insured vehicle is being used for commercial use (MOTOR-DEF-1.3). This exclusion is amended by MOTOR-END-6.1.

### MOTOR-EXC-4.3 Alcohol and drugs
We do not pay for any loss while the driver is under the influence of alcohol above the legal limit, or of drugs.

### MOTOR-EXC-4.4 Breakdown and wear
We do not pay for mechanical or electrical breakdown, failure or wear and tear, unless it results from an incident covered under MOTOR-COV-2.1 or MOTOR-COV-2.5.

### MOTOR-EXC-4.5 Racing
We do not pay for any loss while the insured vehicle is used for racing, pace-making or speed testing.

## MOTOR-CON-5 Conditions

### MOTOR-CON-5.1 Notice of claim
The policyholder must tell us about any incident within 7 days of it happening.

### MOTOR-CON-5.2 Theft reporting
A theft claim is payable only if the theft was reported to the law-enforcement authority within 48 hours of the policyholder discovering it.

### MOTOR-CON-5.3 Admission of liability
The policyholder must not admit liability or offer payment to a third party without our written consent.

## MOTOR-END-6 Endorsements
The endorsements in this section form part of every policy issued under this wording.

### MOTOR-END-6.1 Cost-sharing journeys
MOTOR-EXC-4.2 does not apply when passengers only share the fuel and toll costs of a journey and the driver makes no profit from it.
